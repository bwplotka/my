## About Bartek

Bartłomiej (Bartek) Płotka is a Senior Software Engineering Tech Lead at Google Cloud in London (originally from Gdańsk, Poland), focusing on cloud observability, software efficiency, Go, Rust, and Prometheus.

![about](/og-images/about.jpg)

Since 2012, he has been building and running distributed systems, backend services, and cloud infrastructure. Even with years of experience as a global-scale SRE, he loves software engineering the most: designing pragmatic, fast, and maintainable systems that solve real production problems.

After three years at Intel working on resource management and orchestration in C++ and Go, he joined [Improbable](https://improbable.io) in London to build global Kubernetes infrastructure. In late 2017, he and Fabian Reinartz created the open-source [Thanos](https://thanos.io) project, which became a widely adopted choice for scaling Prometheus globally. That pulled him full-time into open-source observability, joining the [Prometheus](https://prometheus.io) team in 2019 and spending three years as a Principal Software Engineer at [Red Hat](https://redhat.com) working on OpenShift Monitoring, Prometheus, and Thanos.

In 2023, Bartek joined Google Cloud as Tech Lead for Google Cloud Managed Service for Prometheus (GMP). He leads the technical roadmap and collector/query architecture for managed Prometheus across GKE and Cloud Run, while working upstream to modernize Prometheus and its ecosystem. Lately, his work spans three areas:
- Evolving core observability standards and storage engines (Prometheus 3.x, Remote Write 2.0, Native Histograms, [OpenMetrics 2.0](https://github.com/prometheus/OpenMetrics), and Prometheus + OpenTelemetry interoperability).
- Designing next-generation, low-overhead telemetry pipelines using Rust and Apache Arrow ([otel-arrow](https://github.com/open-telemetry/otel-arrow)), alongside on-demand telemetry architectures that dynamically adjust high-cardinality debugging data for engineers and AI agents.
- Building agentic AI engineering workflows for code review, bug triage, benchmarking, and automated vulnerability remediation.

In 2022, Bartek published [Efficient Go](/book) with O'Reilly Media, a book on pragmatic, data-driven performance optimization in Go. In 2026, he was elected to the inaugural [Prometheus Steering Committee](https://github.com/prometheus/governance), where he co-authored Governance 2.0 and the unified [Prometheus Proposal Process (PROM-1)](https://github.com/prometheus/proposals). Previously, he co-founded and served as a Tech Lead for the [CNCF Technical Advisory Group (TAG) for Observability](https://github.com/cncf/tag-observability).

Bartek cares deeply about mentoring, teaching, speaking at conferences, and growing open-source communities. Over the years, he has mentored 30+ engineers through [CNCF LFX](https://github.com/cncf/mentoring) and Google Summer of Code (GSoC) programs, with many becoming core open-source maintainers and conference speakers.

> Every year, I mentor others (and motivate others to mentor!) in the [CNCF](https://github.com/cncf/mentoring) programs. If you would like to be a mentor (or mentee) and help to grow the next generation of open source engineers, [let me know](/)! I am incredibly grateful to those who helped me to be where I am with (often implicit) mentorship. I think we have a duty to help others too.

Outside of work, Bartek values time with his family (wife, daughter, and son), the gym, volleyball, and sometimes not-so-eco-friendly cars or motorcycles (:
